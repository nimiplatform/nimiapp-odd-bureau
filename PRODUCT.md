# 奇物局 — development brief

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

This is a non-authoritative development brief. Approved App gameplay rules are in `.nimi/spec/playground.authority.yaml`; platform execution, identity, capability and lifecycle ownership remain Nimi-owned.

## Platform

web

## Users

Working assumption delegated by the user's open brief: curious people who want a short, surprising game made from their own ordinary surroundings.

## Product Purpose

Turn a photo into a playable world of everyday objects. The approved second iteration compares a strange sound machine with a consequential strike negotiation, while retaining the existing photo mystery. Fun, replayability and something AI uniquely enables are the user's stated acceptance criteria.

## Operating Context

A real Nimi App developed with app-tools and the public SDK/Kit, running in the Desktop-supervised Electron carrier. Chinese is the initial product language.

## Capabilities and Constraints

`vision.locate` supplies real normalized image coordinates. Text generation supplies validated machine plans, actor offers, proposals, stories and mysteries. Machine note transformations execute the round's fixed App-owned rules and are performed locally as sound; optional Runtime speech reads a story. Runtime owns AI execution, routing and availability. No hardcoded provider/model, authored hotspot substitutes, fake success, or silent demo fallback.

## Product Principles

- A photo is the actual game board.
- Connect, run, listen and reroute; or propose, confirm, honor commitments and perform. Each activity has its own complete short loop.
- Stable rules and accepted promises keep the player's choices meaningful; AI expression cannot retroactively change their consequences.
- Any built-in image is a sample scene, never a precomputed AI result.
- The story is fictional and playful; visible evidence and the final answer must agree.
- Preserve play progress across reloads using the admitted Kit shell storage surface.
